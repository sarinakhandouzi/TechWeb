About TechWeb

TechWeb is a technology blog and digital magazine that explains how modern technology works, in plain language.

Our mission

To make technology understandable. We believe anyone curious enough can learn how AI, software and the web work, if the explanations are honest and clear.

Main topics

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Programming and Software Engineering
  • Web Development
  • Autonomous Systems

Why technology education matters

Software shapes work, science and daily life. People who understand it can build better tools, ask better questions and judge new technologies critically instead of treating them as magic.

Editorial vision

Accuracy over hype, examples over jargon, and fundamentals over fashion. Every article should leave the reader able to do or explain something new.
